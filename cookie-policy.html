Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, which can make it easier to visit the site again and make the site more useful to you.

At Apartment Zero Waste Guide, we use cookies to improve your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and show relevant advertisements. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control or delete cookies.

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, secure access to certain areas of the website, and form submission. Without these cookies, the website cannot function correctly.

Examples:

  • Session cookies that keep you logged in during your visit
  • Security cookies that protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF)
  • Load balancing cookies that distribute traffic across servers
  • Cookie consent preference cookies (remember your cookie choices)

Legal basis: These cookies do not require your consent as they are strictly necessary for the website to provide services you have requested.

2. Analytics Cookies (Performance)

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve the website's performance and user experience.

We may use:

  • Google Analytics (if implemented)
  • Other third-party analytics services

Information collected:

  • Number of visitors to the website
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Traffic source (how you found our website)
  • Device and browser information
  • Geographic location (country/city level)

Legal basis: These cookies require your consent before being activated.

3. Advertising Cookies (Marketing)

Advertising cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. If we participate in advertising programs like Google AdSense, these cookies may be placed on your device.

Potential advertising partners:

  • Google AdSense (if implemented)
  • Other advertising networks

Information collected:

  • Browsing history across multiple websites
  • Interests and preferences inferred from browsing behavior
  • Demographic information (age, gender, interests)
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)

Legal basis: These cookies require your explicit consent before being activated.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, but we disclose their presence so you can make informed decisions.

Common third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Embedded videos (YouTube, Vimeo)
  • Social media sharing buttons
  • External fonts (Google Fonts)
  • Analytics and advertising providers

How to Control Cookie Settings

Through Our Cookie Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies: Consents to all types of cookies including analytics and advertising
  • Reject non-essential cookies: Only necessary cookies will be used
  • Customize preferences: Choose which types of cookies you want to allow

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer.

Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

You can also:

  • Delete cookies that have already been set
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies (not recommended, as this may break website functionality)
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set

Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of personalized advertising from major advertising networks:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative
  • Your Online Choices (EU)

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable or reject certain cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

  • Disabling necessary cookies: The website may not work at all
  • Disabling analytics cookies: We won't be able to improve the website based on user behavior
  • Disabling advertising cookies: You'll still see ads, but they won't be relevant to your interests

Even if you disable cookies, you may still see advertisements, but they won't be tailored to your interests.

Google AdSense and Cookies

If we participate in the Google AdSense program, Google may use cookies to serve ads on our website. Here's what you should know:

  • Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads relevant to your interests
  • You can opt out of the DART cookie by visiting Google Ads Settings
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to our website and other sites on the Internet
  • Google's privacy policy covers their use of cookies: Google Privacy Policy

Important: As of the date of this policy, we have not yet activated Google AdSense. If and when we do, we will update this policy and ensure proper consent mechanisms are in place.

International Data Transfers

  • Google Analytics may process data in the United States
  • Advertising networks may operate globally

When data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions by the European Commission
  • Privacy Shield frameworks (where applicable)
