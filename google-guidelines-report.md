## Items That Are Official Google Policies

1. **Content must be original and valuable** - Cannot be scraped, spun, or auto-generated without value
2. **Site must be complete before applying** - No under construction pages
3. **Clear navigation required** - Users must find content easily
4. **Privacy Policy mandatory** - Must disclose data collection practices
5. **No prohibited content** - Adult, violent, hateful, illegal content forbidden
6. **No deceptive practices** - Clickbait, misleading titles, fake claims prohibited
7. **Valid traffic only** - No click fraud, bots, or artificial traffic generation
8. **Proper ad placement** - Not near navigation, not disguised as content
9. **Mobile-friendly required** - Must work on all devices
10. **Fast page experience** - Core Web Vitals matter for ranking

## Items That Are Best Practices (Not Strict Requirements)

1. **1000+ words per article** - Editorial choice for quality, not Google minimum
2. **3 images per article** - Visual quality choice, not AdSense requirement
3. **FAQ section in articles** - Useful for GEO, not mandatory
4. **Table in every article** - Editorial structure choice
5. **40 articles total** - Project scope decision, no official minimum
6. **5 categories** - Organizational choice
7. **Specific color palette** - Design preference
8. **Internal linking architecture** - SEO best practice, not strict rule

## Items Specific to This Project

1. **Apartment composting niche focus** - Editorial decision
2. **Beginner-friendly language** - Target audience choice
3. **Renter-focused content** - Niche differentiation
4. **No comments system** - Simplicity and moderation avoidance
5. **Static site architecture** - Performance and hosting flexibility
6. **Pure HTML/CSS/JS** - No frameworks for speed
7. **WebP image format** - Modern optimization choice
8. **Local search implementation** - No external dependencies

## Potential Conflicts Identified

### Conflict 1: FAQPage Structured Data
**Issue:** Google has changed FAQ structured data eligibility over time
**Resolution:** Implement FAQPage schema only when FAQs are visible on the page, monitor for policy changes before AdSense application

### Conflict 2: Word Count Requirements
**Issue:** Prompt specifies 1000+ words, but Google doesn't require specific length
**Resolution:** Maintain 1000+ words as quality standard but document this as editorial choice, not Google requirement

### Conflict 3: Image Requirements
**Issue:** Prompt requires 3 images per article, Google has no such requirement
**Resolution:** Keep images for user experience but ensure they add value, not just fill space
